How It Works

01
Trigger

Pull pending shifts from Google Sheets (times + Maps link).

02
Geo extraction

Expand short link, extract exact latitude/longitude.

03
Environmental polling

Request FortyGuard thermal arrays; poll until ready.

04
AI reasoning

Groq/Llama evaluates the scheduled block and proposes safer windows.

05
Deterministic validation

Output is forced into SAFE / RESCHEDULED / ESCALATED.

06
Write-back + alerts

Update the Sheet and email an executive summary to the PM. Manager approval is the final gate before schedule changes are accepted.

FAQ

Does this replace dispatchers?

No. It’s Human‑in‑the‑Loop: it proposes, managers approve.

What data do you store?

Shift times, jobsite location via Maps link, and decisions written back to the sheet.

What if the thermal array isn’t ready yet?

The system polls until FortyGuard finishes processing.

Can it work with other schedulers?

Google Sheets is used as a simple database stand‑in; the pattern is adaptable.

How are rules applied?

Constrained AI reasoning plus deterministic validation (SAFE/RESCHEDULED/ESCALATED).
